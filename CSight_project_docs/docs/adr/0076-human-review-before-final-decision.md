# ADR-0076: Human review precedes every final decision

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-27

## Decision

Policy summaries are workflow guidance, not refund, exchange, fraud, liability, or legal outcomes.
Versioned policy and qualified evidence inform a reviewer, who owns the final business decision.

## Consequences

Sprint 5A intentionally has no approval API, routing automation, override, or final-report output.
